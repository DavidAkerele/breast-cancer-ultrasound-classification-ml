Bibliographic details were then checked against the publisher, PubMed, Manchester Metropolitan University repository, or the paper itself. ## Audit outcome

Nine publications have an identifiable Google Scholar record and an independent bibliographic source. The 2026 Bristow and Yap manuscript is retained because it was supplied for this revision and has an official project repository, but it is labelled as forthcoming rather than presented as an established journal publication. The dissertation now cites each work only for a claim that falls within its stated task. This is more defensible than adding a long author list without connecting the papers to the methods or argument.
