# Results and reproducibility status

**Status: audited local-cohort evidence; not suitable for clinical-performance claims.**

The current EfficientNet-B0 checkpoint was evaluated on 62 held-out images from the repaired BrEaST and OASBUD subject-level splits using the repository's shared preprocessing path on 7 September 2026. It achieved 67.74% accuracy, macro F1 0.6774, and ROC-AUC 0.7419. Its bootstrap 95% intervals are 56.45-79.03% for accuracy and 0.6217-0.8616 for ROC-AUC. The machine-readable record is stored in `outputs/metrics.json`, with per-image probabilities in `outputs/predictions.csv`.

The preserved probabilities also support average precision of 0.6803, a Brier score of 0.2144, malignant precision of 0.6364, and malignant recall of 0.7241. A five-bin reliability diagram and a source-stratified check are generated without retraining. BrEaST accuracy is 0.7813 on 32 images and OASBUD accuracy is 0.5667 on 30 images. The two subsets have different error profiles, but the sample is too small and too confounded by acquisition and reconstruction to assign a cause. These values are stored in `outputs/extended_evaluation.json`.

The same-split internal benchmark is generated by `scripts/benchmark_models.py`. On this small test set, EfficientNet-B0 (67.74% accuracy, ROC-AUC 0.7419) outperformed the custom CNN (64.52%, 0.6708) and ResNet-50 (56.45%, 0.6364). This ranking is exploratory and is not a published-model comparison.

The accompanying split audit passes for BrEaST and OASBUD: no subject identifier crosses partitions. BUSI is excluded because the local files were renamed and converted to square derivatives. The repository can no longer repeat published case-level quality checks against the original release or prove patient separation. The exclusion applies to this local copy, not to the value of BUSI as a public research dataset. The result is evidence for this repaired local cohort only; it is not external, clinical, or diagnostic validation.

## Evaluation records and limitations

The evaluation records include a confusion matrix, ROC and precision-recall curves, a reliability diagram, source-level results, an architecture comparison and bootstrap intervals. These outputs derive from saved predictions and metrics. Archived illustrative or simulated figures are excluded from the measured evidence.

The completed checkpoints retain selected validation points rather than complete epoch histories. Future training runs save an epoch-by-epoch record. Preprocessing improvements, noise robustness and external generalisation require further experiments.
