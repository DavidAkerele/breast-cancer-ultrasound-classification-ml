# Chapter 6: Discussion and Conclusion

The project’s contribution is a reproducible research framework: shared preprocessing, mask discovery, versioned evaluation artifacts, and a non-destructive split audit. It is not a clinically validated classifier.

The immediate next step is to preserve original identifiers, group every patient’s views and masks in a single partition, freeze a test cohort before model selection, and rerun all experiments. The dashboard’s outputs are research visualisations rather than diagnosis, BI-RADS assignment, or clinical decision support.
